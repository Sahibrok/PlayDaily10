🎮 How to Play (10 seconds)
  • Swipe a candy (or tap two neighbors) to swap them.
  • Line up 3 or more of a kind and they pop! New candies rain down.
  • 4-in-a-row and 5-in-a-row pay big bonuses, and chain reactions multiply everything: Chain ×2, ×3… 🔥
  • Only swaps that make a match count as a move — failed swaps are free to try.
  • No moves possible? The board auto-shuffles for free. You can never get stuck.
  • ☀️ Daily: identical starting board + identical refill stream for everyone — pure skill decides the score.
